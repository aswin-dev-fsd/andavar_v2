════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A · THE BRIEF
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The sleeper page. Neither competing chain publishes its scheme list clearly, so
this is unclaimed high-intent search traffic. It is also the page that removes
the fear that stops people telephoning at all. Four sections. Tone: matter-of-
fact, never apologetic about money.


B · SECTION SPEC
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SECTION 1 — PAGE HERO                                   band: COOL
  This is the only page whose hero sits on the cool band — the subject is
  administrative, and the colder ground signals that honestly.
  Aperture top-right, 470px, 45%.
  Breadcrumb: Home / Schemes & insurance
  H1 max-width 15ch: "Schemes and insurance"
  Lede EXACT: "The question people are most embarrassed to ask and most worried
  about. So here it is in plain language, before you have to ask anyone."

  SECTION 2 — THE THREE SCHEMES                           band: GROUND
  Three equal cards, 3 columns desktop, 1 under 860px.
  Each: green tick circle → H3 scheme name → caption tag → body → hairline →
  "BRING WITH YOU" label → three bulleted items with short blue dashes.
  The "bring with you" block is pushed to the card bottom so all three align
  even though the titles run to different lengths.

    1 Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme
      tag: CMCHIS · அரசு காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டம்
      "The Tamil Nadu government scheme. Cataract surgery and a number of other
      eye procedures are covered."
      Bring: Your CMCHIS card · Aadhaar card · Ration card, if you have it
    2 Pensioners Health Scheme
      tag: For retired government employees · ஓய்வூதியர் சுகாதாரத் திட்டம்
      "Covers retired state government employees and their dependants under the
      scheme's terms."
      Bring: Your pensioner ID · Scheme card · A recent pension slip
    3 Star Health Insurance
      tag: Private cover · தனியார் காப்பீடு
      "Cashless treatment subject to your policy terms and prior approval where
      required."
      Bring: Policy card or number · A photo ID · Any referral letter

  SECTION 3 — IF YOU HAVE NO SCHEME                       band: WARM
  Split.
    LEFT  Eyebrow "If you have no scheme"
          H2 EXACT: "You will be told the cost before anything is booked."
          Lede EXACT: "Not after. Not on the day. The consultation fee, what the
          procedure would cost, and what is included — stated up front so you can
          decide without pressure."
    RIGHT White card. H4 "Two minutes on the phone settles it"
          "Tell us which scheme you hold, or that you hold none, and what you
          have noticed about your eyes. We will tell you what a first visit costs
          and what is likely to be covered."
          [Call 04259 221 000] primary

  SECTION 4 — COUNTER QUESTIONS                           band: GROUND
  Narrow column, max-width 760px.
  H2 EXACT: "Questions people actually ask at the counter"
  Five hairline rows, no numbers — this is a set, not a sequence:
    · Do I need a referral to use my scheme?
    · Will I have to pay and claim it back?
    · Does the scheme cover both eyes?
    · What about spectacles afterwards?
    · I am not from Pollachi. Can I still use my scheme?
  Closing caption EXACT: "Scheme rules change. If anything here does not match
  what you have been told, call us — we would rather correct it than have you
  turned away at the counter."

D · PAGE RULES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
· No dark section. This page is administrative; it does not need drama.
· The closing caption about rules changing is not optional. A scheme listed here
  but not honoured at the counter creates a patient being turned away, which is
  worse than no page at all.
· VERIFY WITH THE HOSPITAL before publishing. Scheme coverage came from the
  discovery form and contradicts one directory listing.

A · THE BRIEF

Job three of the site's four: get them through the door. In a town the landmark
does more work than the postcode, so the landmark is the headline. Three
sections. Shortest page on the site, deliberately.

             We are directly opposite."

             Evening 5:00 – 7:00 PM / Sunday closed
    TELEPHONE 04259 221 000 (bold) · 94420 56767
             caption: "Call during consulting hours and someone will answer."

  SECTION 3 — BEFORE YOU COME                             band: WARM
  Split, wide left.
    LEFT  Eyebrow "Before you come"
          H2 EXACT: "Four things that make the visit shorter."
          Four hairline rows, no numbers:
            Bring your glasses — all of them — "Including the old pair you
            stopped using. They tell us more than you would expect."
            Bring any previous prescriptions or reports — "Especially if you have
            been seen elsewhere, or have diabetes and carry a sugar record."
            Bring your scheme or insurance card — "It saves a second trip if
            treatment turns out to be needed."
            Bring someone with you, if you can — "If drops are needed to examine
            the retina, your vision will be blurred for a few hours afterwards.
            You should not drive yourself home."

          caption: "No appointment is needed. If you would prefer a set time,
          call ahead."
