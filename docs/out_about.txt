A · THE BRIEF
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Substantiates the "well equipped" claim and explains, without defensiveness, why
a small single-surgeon practice is a choice rather than a limitation. Four
sections. It is the least urgent page on the site and should feel unhurried.


B · SECTION SPEC
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SECTION 1 — PAGE HERO                                   band: GROUND
  Aperture top-right, 470px, 50%.
  Breadcrumb: Home / About
  H1 max-width 17ch EXACT: "A small hospital that has stayed in one place."
  Lede EXACT: "Shri Andavar Eye Care and Retina Centre has been on Palladam Road
  since 2013, run by the surgeon who founded it."

  SECTION 2 — THE STORY                                   band: GROUND
  Split, wide left.
    LEFT  Three paragraphs:
      1 (lede) EXACT: "There are larger eye hospitals within an hour of here, and
        they do good work. What they cannot offer is the thing a small practice
        gives for free: the same surgeon, every time, for as long as you need him."
      2 EXACT: "Most of the people who walk through the door were sent by someone
        — a neighbour who had their cataract done here, a family doctor who has
        been referring patients for years, a son who searched and read about the
        surgeon before bringing his mother in. Reputation in a town this size is
        slow to build and very hard to fake."
      3 EXACT: "The practice takes retina work seriously enough to put it in its
        own name. Alongside that sits a steady volume of cataract surgery,
        diabetic eye screening, glaucoma monitoring and routine examinations —
        the everyday work that keeps a district seeing."
    RIGHT Blue-tinted card. Eyebrow "In short". Four blocks:
      Founded — "2013, in Pollachi, by Dr. A. Raghuram."
      Focus — "Retina and cataract, with full general eye care."
      Languages — "Tamil and English, throughout — at the counter and in the
                   consulting room."
      Where — "One location, on Palladam Road. No branches, by choice."

  SECTION 3 — HOW WE WORK                                 band: COOL
  Centred heading block. Eyebrow "How we work".
  H2 EXACT: "Three things we hold to."
  Three cards, each with a large blue numeral 01/02/03:
    01 Nothing is recommended that is not needed — "If your eyes are fine, you
       will be told they are fine. An unnecessary procedure is a betrayal of
       thirteen years of trust, and it is not worth it."
    02 You will understand what is happening — "Explanations in the language you
       think in, without medical vocabulary, for as long as it takes. Bring your
       questions written down if that helps."
    03 The cost is stated before you commit — "What the visit costs, what the
       procedure costs, and what your scheme covers — said out loud, in advance,
       every time."

D · PAGE RULES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
· No dark section.
· The 01/02/03 numerals here are a list of three principles, not a sequence, but
  numbering is acceptable because they are explicitly "three things". Do not
  number anywhere the count is not the point.
· Facility photography, when it exists, belongs in section 2 beneath the third
  paragraph as a two-up. It will substantiate "well equipped", which is currently
  asserted rather than shown.
